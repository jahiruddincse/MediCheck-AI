About & Disclaimer

Legal & Medical Disclaimer

“MediCheck AI provides informational decision support based on extracted package information and available reference data. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or prove medicine authenticity. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.”

Project Overview

MediCheck AI was developed as an AI-assisted medicine verification and medication-safety decision-support platform for university presentation.

It helps users extract packaging text, compares that text with a controlled reference dataset, and checks the user’s personal medication profile to flag potential duplicate active ingredients.

MediCheck AI - System Architecture

Technical Architecture Guide

MediCheck AI solves two critical healthcare challenges without making unverified authenticity claims: (1) Automating the extraction of small, printed medicine packaging labels, and (2) Cross-referencing active ingredients against a personal medication profile to alert users of duplicate dosing.

Flutter Mobile App (Client)
  ↓ POST image multipart/form-data
FastAPI Backend (Orchestration)
  ↓
OpenCV (Image Preprocessing: Noise Reduction & Binarization)
  ↓
YOLO (Object Detection: WHERE is the Name, Batch, Expiry?)
  ↓
EasyOCR (Character Recognition: WHAT do the regions say?)
  ↓
AI Structuring (Regex & NER mapping to JSON schema)
  ↓
Controlled Reference DB + Deterministic Rule Engine
  ↓
JSON Response with "Attention Required" / "Consistent Info"
  ↓
Flutter UI Verification & Duplicate Warning Screen
Technical Highlight: "We do not train YOLO from zero. We utilize a pretrained YOLO model fine-tuned on medicine packaging bounding boxes. EasyOCR handles text character extraction."

Chatbot Ethical Guardrails

The MediCheck AI Assistant is strictly programmed with the following limitations:

  • Never diagnoses diseases or conditions.
  • Never prescribes medicines or dosage changes.
  • Never tells users to take or stop taking medications.
  • Never claims a medicine is 100% genuine or fake.
  • Only explains data already extracted by the OCR and reference check.
